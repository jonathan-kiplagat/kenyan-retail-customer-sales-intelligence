# Data Sources and Provenance

## 1. Synthetic retail data (all files in `data/raw/`)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| What | Sales transactions, customers, products, stores, promotions and calendar for the fictional chain **Duka Bora** |
| Created by | `src/data_generation/generate_data.py` (Python, pandas, NumPy) |
| Reproducibility | Fixed seed `RANDOM_SEED = 2024` in `src/config.py`; re-running produces byte-identical CSVs (checked by `tests/test_raw_data.py`) |
| Period | 2024-01-01 to 2025-12-31 |
| Licence | Released with this repository under the MIT licence |
| Real-world status | **Not real.** No record describes an actual retailer, customer, brand or price. Prices and costs are plausible illustrations, not market data |

### Business logic built into the data

Recorded here so every pattern found later can be traced back to a known cause.

- **Store traffic:** Nairobi CBD > Westlands > Mombasa > Nakuru > Kisumu > Thika. Westlands has larger baskets and more card payments.
- **Seasonality:** a December peak, a January dip, and a busier April (Easter) and November. Fridays and Saturdays are busiest. Orders are 20% more likely in the payday window (25th to 3rd) and 25% more likely on the day before a public holiday.
- **Category tilts:** clothing peaks in January, May and September (back to school) and in December. Electronics peaks in November and December. Beverages rise in the hot season and in December.
- **Promotions:** while a campaign runs, its target category is 1.7× more likely to be bought, in about 30% larger quantities.
- **Prices:** a 4% shelf-price and cost review on 1 January 2025. Volume growth is about 3% a year.
- **Customers:** four hidden behaviour segments (one-off, occasional, regular, loyal). These are not exported. About a third of regular and loyal customers stop buying part-way through the period ("drifting away").
- **Payments:** depend on the store and on basket value. Baskets of KES 10,000 or more favour card and bank transfer; baskets under KES 500 favour cash.

### Intentionally injected data-quality issues

These are generated by `src/data_generation/inject_quality_issues.py` with seed `2025`. Every change is logged with its original value in `docs/dq_injection_log.csv` (126 entries). The raw sales file is exactly 3,000 rows: 2,988 genuine lines plus 12 duplicates.

## 2. Public context data: Kenyan public holidays

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| File | `data/external/kenya_public_holidays_2024_2025.csv` (28 rows) |
| Publisher | Government of Kenya: Public Holidays Act (Cap. 110), the Constitution of Kenya 2010 Art. 9(4), and Kenya Gazette special notices issued by the Cabinet Secretary for Interior and National Administration |
| Statutory dates | Public Holidays Act on Kenya Law, https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1912/21/eng@2022-12-31 |
| Special (gazetted) dates | Idd-ul-Fitr 2024-04-10, National Tree Growing Day 2024-05-10, Eid-al-Adha 2024-06-17, Idd-ul-Fitr 2025-03-31, Eid-al-Adha 2025-06-06, State funeral of Raila Odinga 2025-10-17. Each is cited per row in `source_url`. The 2024 Eid-al-Adha notice is the Interior Ministry's copy of the Kenya Gazette (Vol. CXXVI No. 84). The others were confirmed from national news reports quoting the gazette notice |
| Sunday rule | Under the Public Holidays Act, a holiday that falls on a Sunday is observed on the following Monday. This applies to Mashujaa Day 2024 (Monday 21 October) and Madaraka Day 2025 (Monday 2 June). Madaraka Day 2024 fell on a Saturday, so it has no substitute |
| Date accessed | 2026-10-30 |
| How obtained | Compiled by hand from the sources above. Every date's weekday was checked in code (notebook 01) |
| Licence / terms | Kenyan legislation and Kenya Gazette notices are public-domain official government records. News pages were used only to confirm dates and their text is not reproduced |
| How it is joined | `calendar.date` = holiday `date` adds `is_public_holiday` and `holiday_name`. Sales join to the calendar on `transaction_date` |
| Limitations | Only national holidays are included; county-level events are not. Some special-gazette dates were confirmed from reputable secondary reports rather than the gazette PDF itself |

## 3. Data considered but not used

- **KNBS Consumer Price Index / inflation** and **2019 Census county populations:** dropped from scope at the project owner's request (2026-10-30). Because the retail data is synthetic, inflation is represented only by the documented 4% price review. No official statistic is simulated or presented as real.
